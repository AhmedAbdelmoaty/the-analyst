# Vision, identity and game-feel contract
The user wants a **GAME**, not a presentation or quiz: animated illustrated market, continuous spatial scene, comic personalities, player agency, time pressure, reward and consequence. Primary audience adult Arabic-speaking learners, mechanics instantly understandable to a 10-year-old. Egyptian colloquial Arabic, natural humor, not patronizing.

## Experience arc
Curiosity -> Leila's practical business trouble -> Glitch's misleading shortcut -> Dotty's evidence-based intervention -> tactile notebook tutorial -> Glitch steals four pages -> four timed chase/evidence encounters -> synthesize evidence -> meaningful operational plan -> acted result. Target 4–5 minutes on a normal run, without forcing waits or punishing tutorial mistakes.

## Visual north star
Bright coherent premium 2D cartoon ice-cream street; layered sky/cart/stalls/foreground, consistent line weight, lighting, palette and proportions. Existing Leila, **pink Dotty**, **purple Glitch** are canonical. The previously approved 11-panel storyboard is style guidance only; it may not be accessible to Codex: request actual reference if missing, never claim exact reproduction. Do not adopt previously rejected unrelated blue mascot or isolated chart mockups. The chart is a notebook/game object, not a white corporate dashboard. UI as playful tactile paper tabs/stamps/collectibles with excellent legibility.

## Game feel test for every scene
`Player action -> immediate audiovisual response -> character/world consequence -> collected evidence or story advancement -> learning payoff`. Motion must convey mass: anticipation, squash/stretch on impact, overshoot, secondary motion, differentiated poses. Never call sliding/bobbing a flattened PNG 'character acting'. No animations so long that repeated play becomes annoying. Skippable story, responsive input, accessible fallback.

## What cannot change
Core challenge answers are the THREE tap choices only, always available. Four relationships: positive, positive, no clear trend, negative. Story and final plan must reflect all four. Three hearts, 20 seconds each round as initial tuning; no time/lives in tutorial. 3 outcomes: perfect, saved, Glitch win. No random unrelated minigame. Maintain original route and app integration.
